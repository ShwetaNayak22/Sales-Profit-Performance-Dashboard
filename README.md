# 📊 Sales & Profit Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales and profit performance using **Excel, MySQL, Power BI, and DAX** to identify business trends, calculate key performance indicators (KPIs), and generate actionable business insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze sales and profit performance
- Calculate important business KPIs
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Compare regional performance
- Analyze product category performance
- Evaluate customer segment contribution
- Identify the impact of discounts on profitability
- Identify top-performing customers
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Generate business insights and recommendations

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning, formulas, Pivot Tables
- **MySQL** – SQL queries, JOINs, aggregations, business analysis
- **Power BI** – Data visualization, dashboard development, filters and slicers
- **DAX** – KPI calculations, measures, growth analysis

## 💡 Key Insights

- Sales performance varies across different months.
- Regional performance differs significantly across markets.
- Product categories show different sales and profitability patterns.
- Higher discounts can negatively impact profit margins.
- Customer segments contribute differently to overall revenue.
- A small group of customers contributes significantly to total sales.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Recommendations

- Improve performance in underperforming regions.
- Focus on high-growth and profitable product categories.
- Control excessive discounts to protect profit margins.
- Develop targeted strategies for high-value customer segments.
- Improve sales during low-performing months.
- Monitor profitability along with sales growth.

---

## 🏁 Conclusion

The Sales & Profit Performance Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of business performance by combining sales, profit, regional, category, customer, and discount analysis. The insights generated can help management make data-driven decisions, improve profitability, and support sustainable business growth.
